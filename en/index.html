What we do.

Whether you want to sit down to a proper concert, unwind to romantic ballads over a cocktail or dance on the tables to the liveliest party songs, our set list takes you where you want to go.

In our covers of the Nashville greats — Zach Top, Chris Stapleton, Brad Paisley, The Zac Brown Band, The Kruse Brothers, Blake Shelton — we bring the real American spirit on stage: rustic textures, a solid rhythm section, interlacing strings and rich vocal harmonies.

American country, neotraditional and contemporary, from the Nineties onwards, with a few older exceptions — Country Roads by John Denver and Jolene by Dolly Parton.

Where we shine.

Pubs & venues

Two sets, even in a small room: 4 × 3 m and a power socket are enough for us. The volume is set on the room: people listen, sing along and keep chatting.

Ranches & riding clubs

Our natural habitat: hay bales, boots and guitars. Today’s American country, with the songs that get people up on their own.

Feasts & village fairs

Squares, parish grounds and village feasts, from the afternoon into the night. The PA and lights are ours: we load in, play and load out without the organisers lifting a finger.

Festivals

Big stages and quick changeovers: we arrive with everything ready, so the soundcheck does not eat the evening.

Corporate & private parties

Openings, dinners and parties at private villas. A light acoustic set during drinks, the full band after dinner: the volume follows the moment.

Weddings

Ceremony, drinks and party: from ballads under the pergola to party songs on the tables. We learn the song for your entrance on request.

Our line-up.

Guitars and vocals

Charisma and energy. A born frontman, he found in country music the right stage to put technique and sheer passion together.

Outgoing, larger than life, a free spirit made of musical knowledge and honesty.

Drums and vocals

Precision, imagination and balance. A gentle giant, stubborn as a mule.

We could have played that better.

Colour and good spirits. A quiet one — but words are not needed when his hands find melodies that seem to come straight from the heart of Tennessee.
