Everything you ask us.

Fee, running time, PA and lights, space, travel: the answers to the questions we get most often.

What is your fee?

It depends on the size of the event, the travel involved and the PA and lighting you need: every night is different. Fill in the quote form and we’ll get back to you with a proposal within a few days.

How long do you play for?

Our show runs about two hours, in one or two sets. Got a whole day to cover? We add an acoustic set, or we keep the PA playing our own music while the stage is empty.

Do you bring your own PA and lights?

Yes — and it is us running it! 😉 Rigging, cabling, sound and lights: we do all of it. To keep your wallet safe, we are self-sufficient up to an audience of 250/300; above that we bring in the sound company we trust.

Where are you from? How far do you travel?

We are from Brescia, in northern Italy, but we will come and make you dance wherever you are. Available for travel across Italy and abroad. 🕺🏻

Do you play in smaller line-ups?

We play as a five-piece: that is our show line-up. For a lunch, an aperitivo or a whole day to cover we prepare an acoustic set — still the five of us, just different instruments.

How much space do you need?

4 by 3 metres is our minimum.

Do you play a fixed set list?

Yes, the set list is fixed: our show is a concert of about 25 songs. For special occasions, agreed in advance, we are happy to prepare songs on request.

Do you need board and lodging?

A meal for the band, before or after the event, is part of the quote. Lodging depends on the distance and the logistics, and we work it out together. And if a beer is left over, gladly. 🍺

A night with us?

Tell us about your event: two minutes of form, and we’ll get back to you with a proposal within a few days.
